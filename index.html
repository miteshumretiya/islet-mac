Questions

Good to know.

Is Islet really free?

Yes. No trial, no ads, no account and no in-app purchases.

My screen has no notch. Will it work?

Yes. On screens without a notch, like most Windows PCs and external displays, Islet draws its own island at the top centre.

Why does my Mac or PC warn me the first time?

Islet isn't sold through the App Store or Microsoft Store, so Apple and Microsoft show a one-time warning. The steps under each download get you past it once. You can also compare the file's SHA-256 with the one shown there.

What does Islet collect?

Nothing. Everything happens on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. It doesn't even need an internet connection.

Which music apps work?

Anything that shows up in your system's media controls: Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube in your browser, podcasts and more.

How do I quit or uninstall it?

Right-click Islet's tray or menu bar icon and choose Quit. To uninstall, delete the app. On Windows, turn off "Start with Windows" in Islet's settings first.
