Questions

Good to know

Is Islet really free?

Yes. No trial, no ads, no account, no in-app purchases.

My screen has no notch. Will it work?

Yes. On screens without a notch (most Windows PCs, external displays), Islet draws its own island at the top centre. You can choose a floating pill or a notch shape.

Why does my Mac or PC warn me when I open it?

Islet isn't sold through the App Store or Microsoft Store, so Apple and Microsoft show a one-time warning for apps they haven't reviewed. The steps under each download get you past it once; after that it opens normally. You can compare the file's SHA-256 with the one shown above.

What does Islet collect?

Nothing. Everything happens on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. It doesn't even need an internet connection.

Which music apps work?

Anything that shows up in your system's media controls: Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube in your browser, podcasts and more.

How do I quit or uninstall it?

Right-click Islet's tray or menu-bar icon and choose Quit. To uninstall, delete the app. On Windows, turn off "Start with Windows" in Islet's settings first.
